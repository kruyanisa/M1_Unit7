Myths & Legends

Listen and Read Along 🎧📖
Fact or fiction? There are a lot of myths and legends around the world. Are they true? We will never know. However, they still fascinate us.
Hercules and the Lernaean Hydra
🤔 Q: Who was Hercules?
A: He was the son of Zeus, the most powerful of the gods, and Alcmene.
Hercules and the Lernaean Hydra. Hercules lived in Greece. He was the son of Zeus, the most powerful of the gods, and Alcmene. Hercules was very strong. He had to carry out 12 labours to become a god. One of these was to kill the Hydra. The Hydra was a monster with nine heads. It wasn't easy to kill. Each time he tried to cut off one of her heads, two more appeared in its place. With the help of his nephew, Iolaus, Hercules managed to cut off eight of the heads. Then he grabbed the ninth head and buried it under a big rock. The Hydra was dead.
Saint George and the Dragon
🤔 Q: Who was St George?
A: He was a brave soldier.
Saint George and the Dragon. Saint George was a brave soldier. He travelled a lot until he reached Libya, in North Africa. The people there were very sad. A dragon killed all the young girls. Only the king's daughter was still alive. Saint George decided to save the princess. The dragon was very scary. Its head was big and its tail was long. Saint George tried to kill it with his spear, but he failed. He tried again, but still he didn't succeed. In the end, after a long battle, he managed to kill it. The princess and all the people were safe at last.
