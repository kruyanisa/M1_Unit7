Dwayne Johnson
Dwayne Douglas Johnson was born on 2nd May 1972, in California. As a child Dwayne wanted to be an American football player. His dream ended after he was not selected to the draft team. He then decided to focus on his wrestling career and signed a contract with the WWF. He won many titles performing as the Rock, which included ten world championships.

In 2002, Dwayne landed his first leading role in the film The Scorpion King. Since then he has starred in many successful film series like Fast and Furious and Jumanji. Dwayne is now retired from professional wrestling, and focusses on his acting and producing career.
Dwayne is married to Lauren Hashian and he has three children. He is very tall at 196 cm and he weighs 118 kg.
